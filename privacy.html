1. Overview

XM Compass ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal finance companion for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is handled, and the choices you have.

The App was designed from day one to keep your financial data on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your transactions, balances, budgets, or receipts.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data from you. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • Financial account numbers, credentials, or bank connections.
  • Location data, device identifiers, or advertising IDs.
  • Transaction records, balances, budgets, or goals.
  • Receipt photos, notes, or tags you create in the App.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you create in XM Compass — accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, receipt photos, notes, tags, and preferences — is stored locally on your iPhone inside the App's private sandbox. This data:

  • Never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.
  • Is included in your standard iOS device backups if you back up your iPhone, subject to Apple's backup settings.
  • Can be removed at any time by deleting the App or by using the in-app "Wipe All Data" feature in Settings.

You can export your data to CSV or JSON from the App's Settings screen. Those export files are created and saved by you, on your device, and are not transmitted to us.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or backend service that would receive your data. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in the App that profile users or track behavior across apps.

Apple may collect certain diagnostic data according to Apple's own privacy policy when you use the App Store or iOS itself. That collection is governed by Apple, not by us.

5. Permissions We Request

XM Compass only requests the minimum iOS permissions needed for the features you use:

  • Camera — used solely to capture receipt photos that you choose to attach to a transaction. Photos are stored locally.
  • Photo Library — used solely to pick existing photos to attach as receipts. We do not upload them anywhere.
  • Location (optional, transaction-level) — if you choose to attach a location to a transaction, the App reads your current location once. The location is saved with that transaction locally.

You can deny any of these permissions and the App will continue to work; features that depend on the denied permission will simply be unavailable.

6. Analytics

XM Compass does not use first-party or third-party analytics tools. We do not measure usage, retention, conversion, or any other behavior. There is no telemetry, no event logging, and no remote reporting of any kind.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because we collect nothing from anyone, this policy applies uniformly to all users regardless of age.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side. You have full control over the data on your device:

  • Export — Settings → Data → Export to CSV or JSON.
  • Wipe — Settings → Data → Wipe All Data removes every record from the App.
  • Delete the App — removing the App from your device removes all of its local data.
  • Revoke Permissions — you can change camera, photo, or location access at any time in iOS Settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App. Because no account is required, we cannot notify you by email.

What it does

Built for the way you actually live

Every screen is designed to give you a useful answer in under five seconds — no menus, no clutter, no learning curve.

◎

Focused Dashboard

Three numbers up front: net position, period income, and period spending. Underneath, live budgets, accounts, a category donut, and your latest activity.

+

Quick Capture

Add income, expense, or transfer in seconds. Pick a category, attach up to six receipt photos, add notes, tags, or a location.

▦

Envelope Budgets

Set a monthly limit per category. A burndown curve compares your actual pace to an ideal line, with alerts when you cross your own threshold.

◇

Goal Tracking

Pick a name, target, and date. XM Compass computes your daily save, milestones appear automatically, and rings update the moment you contribute.

▤

Accounts

Cash, checking, savings, and credit cards — with running balances that respect transfers in and out.

▥

Insights & Analytics

Cash-flow trends, top merchants, category breakdowns, and a heatmap of your daily activity — drawn in SwiftUI Canvas.

↗

Export Anytime

Two-tap CSV or JSON export. Your data, your format, your file. Nothing held back.

◐

Dark-First Design

Calm by default. Easy on the eyes at night, easy to read in the morning. Designed for everyday use.

Your data stays on your iPhone.

XM Compass does not connect to your bank, does not require an account, and does not sync to any server. All transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, attachments, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

FAQ

Common questions

Does XM Compass connect to my bank?
No. The app is fully manual and offline. You record transactions yourself, and nothing is fetched from any financial institution.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no signup, no login, and no email collection. Install the app and start using it.
Where is my data stored?
All data is stored locally on your device. Receipt photos are stored in the app's private sandbox.
Can I export my data?
Yes. From Settings, you can export everything to CSV or JSON. You can also wipe all data with a single confirmation.
Which devices are supported?
XM Compass runs on iPhone with iOS 16 or later. It is iPhone-only and optimized for a dark-first experience.

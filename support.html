Frequently asked questions

How do I add a transaction?
Tap the floating + button on the Dashboard or Transactions tab. Choose Income, Expense, or Transfer, enter the amount, pick a category, optionally attach a receipt photo, and save.
Where is my data stored?
Everything is stored locally on your iPhone. There is no server, no account, and no sync. Your data is included in your standard iOS device backups if you have iCloud or iTunes backup enabled.
How do I back up my data?
The simplest way is to keep your regular iPhone backups enabled (iCloud or iTunes/Finder). For an extra copy, go to Settings → Data → Export and save the CSV or JSON file somewhere safe.
Can I move my data to a new iPhone?
Yes — restore from your iCloud or iTunes backup on the new device. XM Compass data lives inside the App's local storage and is included in the backup. If you want a manual transfer, export to JSON, copy the file to the new device, then re-import (where supported).
How do I export my data?
Open Settings → Data → Export and choose CSV or JSON. The file is generated on your device and saved to a share location you control.
How do I delete all my data?
Open Settings → Data → Wipe All Data and confirm. This permanently removes every transaction, account, budget, goal, and receipt from the App. You can also delete the App itself to remove everything.
How do budgets work?
Budgets use a monthly envelope per category. Set a limit, and the burndown chart compares your actual pace against an ideal line. You'll see a warning when you cross your own threshold.
How do goals work?
Pick a name, a target amount, and a date. XM Compass computes how much you need to save per day, lays out milestones, and the progress ring updates the moment you log a contribution.
The app feels slow with a lot of data. What can I do?
If you've been using the App for a long time, exporting to JSON and using Settings → Data → Wipe All Data (then re-importing if needed) can sometimes help. If the issue persists, please email us with your device model and iOS version.
Does the App support iPad or Mac?
XM Compass is iPhone-only and requires iOS 16 or later. It is not currently available for iPad or Mac.
How do I request a feature or report a bug?
Send us an email at bidihefeg866@gmail.com. For bugs, please include your device model, iOS version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are always appreciated.
